Quick copy

Auto mode

Optional. When on, ApplyPilot works through the Saved jobs in your tracker one at a time in a background tab and clicks Submit for you, but only when every required field is filled, nothing needs your answer (salary, personal, extra files, unknown) and there is no CAPTCHA or sign-in. Everything else moves to Needs review. LinkedIn is never automated. Review mode (Autofill tab) still leaves Submit to you.

Find jobs automatically

When the queue is empty, Auto mode finds fresh entry-level jobs that match your profile (Greenhouse and Lever forms first, then other boards), adds them to your tracker as Saved · Auto and applies with the same safety checks. No AI credits are used to find them. Leave the fields blank to use your profile.

Auto mode needs site access to open job pages in the background. Allow all sites (recommended, employers use many domains) or only the main job boards.

Zero-click Auto-apply (recommended jobs)

Applies to the jobs each board recommends for you, inside your signed-in Chrome, one at a time. Same safety gates: daily caps, 25–75 s pauses, stops on CAPTCHA, sign-in wall or limit message, and unsure answers go to Needs review. LinkedIn is never automated. Needs Auto mode on.

Settings are edited on the website. Edit Auto-apply settings ↗
